The Luncheon

Join us in Wrigleyville

Every February, Phi Psis everywhere mark the founding of our Fraternity on February 19, 1852. This year Chicago-area alumni will celebrate the 175th with an afternoon of lunch, fellowship, and recognition of the brothers who carry the Fraternity forward.

Whatever your chapter or class year, and whether it's been two years or forty since you were last at an alumni event, you're welcome. Bring a brother.

Chicago's Theme · Phi Psi Now

The Chicago Standard

Last year we said Phi Psi Forward. We restored traditions, started new ones, and put the tools in place. Now the work is here. These are our commitments for the year ahead, and the bar we're setting as we work to become the best alumni association in Phi Kappa Psi.

Commitment 1

Programming That Helps Us Flourish

Professional development for undergraduates and alumni alike, community service that puts the Great Joy of Serving Others into practice, and social events that build and strengthen the bonds of brotherhood.

Commitment 2

From Chapter to Alumni

Alumni showing up for the chapters across our area as mentors and advisors, keeping established alumni engaged, and making sure graduating seniors and young alumni find their place in the association from the day they graduate.

Commitment 3

Engaged Across the Fraternity

A Chicago that shows up for Phi Kappa Psi nationally: supporting fellow alumni associations, contributing to national committees, and championing the Fraternity's programs alongside its staff and leadership.

“The Great Joy of Serving Others.”
